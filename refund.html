Refund Policy

Last updated: August 4, 2026

We want you to be confident about your purchase, and we know LEDsplay depends on hardware you assemble yourself, so we offer a straightforward refund window.

1. 14-day money-back guarantee

If you're not satisfied with LEDsplay for any reason, you can request a full refund within 14 days of your purchase date - no detailed explanation required.

Before buying, we recommend trying the free tier (which never expires) to confirm LEDsplay works with your MIDI piano, and using the 14-day trial of paid features to test the full feature set on your own hardware.

2. How to request a refund

Refunds are processed through Lemon Squeezy, our payment processor and merchant of record. You can request one by:

  • Replying to your Lemon Squeezy purchase receipt email, or
  • Using the "Request a refund" option in your Lemon Squeezy order/customer portal

Please use these official Lemon Squeezy channels for refund requests rather than GitHub - our GitHub Issues are for product questions and bug reports, and aren't monitored for order or payment matters.

Approved refunds are returned to your original payment method and typically appear within 5–10 business days, depending on your bank or card provider.

3. After a refund

Once a refund is issued, your license key is deactivated and paid features stop working - the software reverts to the free tier. Hardware you've assembled is yours to keep; we don't sell or take back physical components.

4. After 14 days

Refund requests made after the 14-day window are evaluated case by case - reach out through the same Lemon Squeezy channels above and we'll do our best to help.

5. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent by email to ledsplay@pawel.build. Bug reports and product questions are also welcome on GitHub Issues.
