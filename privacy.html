Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 4, 2026

This policy explains what data LEDsplay (developed by Paweł Gajo, "we", "us") collects and how it's used. LEDsplay is designed to run entirely on your own Raspberry Pi with no cloud dependency, so there is very little to disclose.

1. What we collect

Locally, on your device: when you install LEDsplay, the software generates a random device identifier (a UUID) stored only on your Raspberry Pi. This identifier is used solely to activate and validate your software license - it is not linked to any personal information by us, and never leaves your device except as described below.

License validation: when you activate a paid license, your device identifier and license key are sent to Lemon Squeezy (our payment and license processor) to verify the license is valid and within its activation limit. This is the only network request LEDsplay's license system makes to a third party.

Purchase information: if you buy a license, payment and billing details (name, email, payment method) are collected and processed directly by Lemon Squeezy under their own privacy policy. We do not receive or store your payment card details.

2. What we don't collect

LEDsplay does not include analytics, tracking pixels, or third-party advertising SDKs, either in the application or on this website. We do not sell or share your data with third parties beyond the license validation described above.

3. Update checks

LEDsplay may periodically check GitHub's public release feed to see if a newer version is available. This is a standard anonymous HTTP request to GitHub and does not transmit any identifying information about you or your device.

4. Data you control

Any songs, recordings, or settings you upload stay locally on your Raspberry Pi and are never transmitted anywhere. Uninstalling LEDsplay or reflashing your SD card removes all locally stored data.

5. Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised via GitHub Issues.
